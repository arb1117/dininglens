DiningLens Privacy Policy

Effective [DATE] · Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

DiningLens helps you estimate the calories and macros in your meals. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and what stays on your phone.

No account, no name

DiningLens does not ask for your name, email address, or a password. When you install the app it creates a random install ID. The app sends this ID with each request to our server so we can apply usage limits. It is not linked to your identity.

What stays on your phone

Your meal log, goals, body profile (such as height, weight, age, and activity level), weight entries, saved foods, and water and exercise logs are stored only on your device. We do not keep a copy. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What is sent off your phone, and why

What our server keeps

Our server keeps your install ID and daily usage counts in memory to enforce limits. This is erased whenever the server restarts. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived request logs (time, route, and error messages) for troubleshooting. We do not store your photos or messages.

Third parties

Data sent to Anthropic, Google, USDA, and Open Food Facts is handled under their own privacy policies. Anthropic does not use API inputs to train its models by default. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

Permissions

Camera: to photograph meals and scan barcodes. Photo library: to analyze meal photos you choose. Location: to detect a nearby dining hall or restaurant. Each permission is optional; the app works without it, with fewer features.

Children

DiningLens is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

You can turn off camera, photo, or location access in your phone's Settings at any time. You can clear today's log in the app, and deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. Because we do not keep your meal data, there is nothing on our side to request or delete beyond the in-memory usage counts described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the app's release notes.
